**Oinkoin Free now supports in-app Pro purchases**

Good news! The free Oinkoin app on Google Play now lets you unlock all Pro features with a single one-time in-app purchase.

To support Google Play's billing library, the free app's APK now includes the `INTERNET` permission. It is used **only** to process that purchase.

Your privacy is unchanged:
- All your data stays stored **offline** on your device
- There is **no telemetry** and nothing is ever uploaded
- Oinkoin is completely **open source**

If the internet permission is a concern for you, you can still buy the standalone **Oinkoin Pro** app on Google Play — it contains no internet permission at all.

[Read more in our blog](https://oinkoin.com/blog/pro-features-now-in-app)
