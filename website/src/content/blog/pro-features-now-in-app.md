# Oinkoin Pro Features — Now Available In-App in the Free App

<div style="text-align:center; margin: 2em 0;">
  <img src="/oinkoin-icon.png" alt="Oinkoin Icon" style="width:96px; height:96px; display:inline-block;" />
</div>

We have some exciting news: **all Oinkoin Pro features are now available as a one-time in-app purchase directly inside the free Android Oinkoin app.**

That means you can unlock everything Pro has to offer — backups and restore, recurring records, multiple wallets, budgets, extra icons and colors, custom date ranges, tags, PDF export and more — without downloading a second app.

## Why We Did This

Until now, getting Pro features meant installing a separate app. If you were already using the free app, that came with friction:

- A second install (and a second icon wondering which one to open).
- Manually exporting a backup from one app and importing it into the other.
- The risk of losing a record or two somewhere along the way.

With Pro built into the app you already use, all of that disappears. **One app, one purchase** — and your data simply stays where it is.

## What This Means for Existing Oinkoin Pro Users

Nothing changes unless you want it to:

- **The separate Oinkoin Pro app stays.** It will continue to receive regular updates, and everything you've paid for keeps working exactly as before.
- Both apps remain fully supported side by side. Use whichever fits you best.
- Your data is not touched by this change in any way.

## How to Get Pro Features in the Free App

1. Open the **free Oinkoin app** on Android.
2. Go to **Settings → Premium** (or tap any Pro feature).
3. Complete the one-time **Lifetime Pro** purchase through Google Play.

The upgrade applies instantly, on this device and any device where you restore purchases with the same Google account.

## F-Droid Users

This change does not affect the F-Droid version of Oinkoin. The F-Droid build remains fully featured as always, with no billing involved.

## FAQ

**Does the app depend on Google Play services or Google API?**
The free version of Oinkoin includes the BILLING permission in its manifest to support in-app purchases. While the app can function completely without Google Play Services, you will need them if you wish to upgrade to Pro. The standalone Pro and F-Droid versions do not contain any billing code. Rest assured, your privacy and experience remain unchanged!

**Is my data affected?**
No. Buying Pro inside the free app doesn't move, export, or modify any of your records.

**Will the Oinkoin Pro app be removed?**
No. It stays on Google Play and continues to receive regular updates.

**I already own Oinkoin Pro. Do I need to buy again?**
If you want to have Pro features on the free Oinkoin app, please reach out to us via email.
